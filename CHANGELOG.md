# Aureole Cursor – Changelog

---

## 0.4.2

First public version.

### New
- Main Ring, GCD Ring, Cast Ring and a center dot around the mouse cursor. Cast, channel and empowered spells are shown on the Cast ring.
- Optional background track behind the GCD and Cast ring.
- Mouse trail with rainbow, class or custom color, adjustable duration, spacing and size, optional shrinking.
- Main Ring turns to a combat color automatically in combat (can be switched off, color is adjustable).
- Visibility options: overall opacity, opacity out of combat, show in combat only, hide while mouse-looking, frame layer.
- One settings page with a section per element. Open it with `/ac` or `/aureole`.
- English and German.

### Changed
- Ring textures come in 64, 128 and 256 pixel versions and are chosen by the on-screen size, so small rings stay sharp.
- The color picker is greyed out while class color is active.
